# Divergence Live Wallpaper — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-10-01

**Developer:** Corey Flynn **Contact:** corrifa@gmail.com

This is the privacy policy for the Android app **Divergence Live Wallpaper** (package `com.divergencelivewallpaper`), including its live wallpaper, screensaver and home screen widgets. It explains what information the app handles, why, and what your choices are.

## Summary

Divergence draws an animated particle wallpaper on your device. It has no accounts, no logins, and no servers operated by the developer. The only third-party data flows are the **Google AdMob** banner shown to free users in the app (never on the wallpaper itself) and purchases of **Premium**, which are handled by **Google Play**.

## What the app stores on your device

The app itself does **not** collect personally identifying information. It does not request your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.

The following are stored only on your device:

- **Settings and presets:** your wallpaper settings and the presets you save, including names you give them.
- **Premium status:** whether you have unlocked Premium, so the app works offline.

**Touches** on your home screen or in the app are used only to move the particles in real time. They are not recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. **Clock mode** reads your device's time and 12/24-hour setting to draw the clock; this also stays on your device.

### Device backup

If you have Android's backup to your Google account turned on, Android may include the app's settings, presets and Premium status in that backup so they return when you restore or set up a new device. This backup is managed by Google under your account; the developer has no access to it.

### Importing and exporting presets

Premium users can export presets to a file and import them from one. The app reads or writes **only the file you choose** in Android's file picker, and only when you ask it to. Where that file goes afterwards (for example, a cloud drive or an email) is up to you.

## Purchases (Google Play)

Premium is a one-time purchase made through **Google Play Billing**. Google processes the payment; the developer never receives your payment details. The app receives only the information it needs to confirm that you own Premium (the purchase status and a purchase token from Google Play), and stores your Premium status on your device.

Purchases are governed by the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Ads (Google AdMob)

Free users see a banner ad inside the app's preview and settings screens. Ads are never shown on the wallpaper, the screensaver, or the widgets, and Premium removes them.

The banner is served by the **Google AdMob** SDK. When an ad is requested, AdMob may collect:

- Your device's resettable **advertising ID**.
- Your **IP address**, used coarsely for ad serving and fraud prevention.
- Ad-interaction events (which ad was shown, whether it was tapped).
- General device information (model, OS version, screen size, language).

On devices that support it, AdMob may also use Android's Privacy Sandbox ad features (such as Topics and Attribution) in place of, or alongside, the advertising ID.

This data is sent to Google and is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

For details on the data AdMob handles and how to limit it, see: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

## Consent (EU / UK / Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app uses Google's **User Messaging Platform** to ask for your consent before ads are requested. Your choice controls whether AdMob can serve personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from **Settings → Ad privacy** in the app.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings (**Settings → Privacy → Ads** on most devices).

## Permissions

The app and the Google libraries it uses request these permissions:

- **Internet and network state:** to load ads and to connect to Google Play for purchases.
- **Advertising ID and ad services:** used by AdMob as described above.
- **Google Play billing:** to make and restore the Premium purchase.
- **Background work (wake lock, foreground service):** used by the Google ads library to schedule its own tasks.

The app does not request location, contacts, storage, camera, or microphone permissions.

## Deleting your data

Because the app keeps its data on your device, you can delete all of it by uninstalling the app or by clearing its storage (**Settings → Apps → Divergence Live Wallpaper → Storage → Clear storage**). Data held by Google (ads, purchases, device backups) is managed through your Google account: https://myaccount.google.com

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
